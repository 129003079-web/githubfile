SASTRA Deemed University, located in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, is a premier institution known for its excellence in engineering, law, management, and sciences. Established in 1984, it has grown into a multidisciplinary university offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs. The campus is equipped with modern laboratories, a vast library, and vibrant student activity centers, fostering both academic and extracurricular growth. Its emphasis on research, industry collaboration, and holistic education has earned it a strong reputation across India.
